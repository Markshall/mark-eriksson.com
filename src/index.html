    Senior Full-Stack Developer · Crown Oil Ltd
    
            
    Jul 2018 — now
    
          
    
          
    
            Sole lead developer for the company’s main website and its sister companies, plus several internal systems.
            I build every site from scratch as a custom WordPress theme, on a shared back-end library I wrote for form
            handling, phone and email verification and common utilities. Calculators and converters are HTML custom
            elements that any of the sites can embed in their own colours. Quote requests flow into the company’s CRMs.
            I moved the whole group onto Cloudflare, in front of AWS, and tuned its caching, which made the sites far
            more reliable.
